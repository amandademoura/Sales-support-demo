TOMATO SOUP WITH GRILLED CHEESE
Serves 4

===================
TOMATO SOUP
===================

INGREDIENTS
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 (28 oz) cans whole peeled tomatoes
- 2 cups vegetable or chicken broth
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1/2 tsp dried basil (or a few fresh leaves)
- 1/2 cup heavy cream (optional, for creamy version)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and
   cook until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic and cook for another 30 seconds, until fragrant.
3. Add the canned tomatoes (with their juices), broth, sugar, and
   basil. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon.
4. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 20-25 minutes, stirring
   occasionally.
5. Blend the soup until smooth using an immersion blender, or carefully
   transfer to a countertop blender in batches.
6. Stir in the cream if using. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
7. Keep warm while you make the grilled cheese.

===================
GRILLED CHEESE
===================

INGREDIENTS (per sandwich)
- 2 slices sandwich bread
- 1-2 tbsp butter, softened
- 2-3 slices cheddar cheese (or a mix of cheddar and mozzarella)

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Butter one side of each bread slice.
2. Place one slice butter-side down in a skillet over medium-low heat.
3. Layer the cheese on top, then top with the second slice, butter-side
   facing up.
4. Cook for 3-4 minutes, until the bottom is golden brown.
5. Flip carefully and cook another 3-4 minutes, until the second side
   is golden and the cheese is fully melted.
6. Remove, let rest for a minute, then slice diagonally.

===================
TO SERVE
===================
Ladle the tomato soup into bowls and serve with the grilled cheese
alongside, or cut into strips for dipping.

NOTES
- For extra flavor, roast the tomatoes with garlic before blending.
- A pinch of red pepper flakes adds a nice kick to the soup.
- Sourdough or a sturdy white bread works best for grilled cheese.
